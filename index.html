How it works

  1. Make your card in the Ala app, with your own photo and words. Or make it on the screen of any Ala kiosk.
  2. Print it at an Ala kiosk inside a gift shop, in about five minutes.
  3. Pay at the shop’s counter and hand it over today.
  4. The app also reminds you of birthdays, and with Ask, your friends can add their own dates.

More than 13,000 cards printed at our kiosks, and the most popular design is the blank one, because people would rather say it themselves.
