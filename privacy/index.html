Privacy policy

Effective 24 October 2026

This policy explains what Ala collects on the website (www.alacard.io) and in the Ala app, why, and how to have it deleted. Ala is made by M/S KADO, which runs self-service card-printing kiosks in shops across India.

What we collect

When you use Ala, these reach our servers:

  • Your mobile number: to sign you in with a one-time code, and to find your cards when you enter it at a kiosk.
  • Your account ID: a number we give your account.
  • The cards you save, including any photo you add and the words you write, so a kiosk can print them for you.
  • If you ask someone for their special days: your name, as you give it in Me. It is shown to them on the link.
  • If someone sends you an Ala link to add your special days: what you choose to enter (your name, birthday, anniversary and, if you like, your address). Your address is private unless you choose to share it. See "Your days and address" below.
  • For a card that comes alive: see "Cards that come alive" below.
  • Anything you write in a feedback box.
  • How many times each card that comes alive is opened (a count, with no personal data).

What stays on your phone or in your browser

These are kept only on your device and are never sent to us:

  • In the app: your name, email address and saved addresses, if you add them; your reminders; and the names and numbers you pick into People.
  • On the website: designs you "Keep for later", which stay in your browser until you send them to a kiosk or remove them.
  • Your location. If you allow it, Ala uses it only on your device to sort the kiosk list nearest first. It is not sent anywhere or stored.

Cards that come alive

These are public by design. When such a card prints, a picture of its front, the first name typed in it (on Balloons and Safari cards) and its secret video are stored under the code inside the card. Anyone who has the card can see them by scanning it. The person who sent it can see whether it has been watched. Each card has one video, which can be added up to 30 days after printing.

Your days and address (when someone asks for them)

If you fill in an Ala link sent by a friend or family member, we keep what you enter so that they are reminded of your days. If you add an address, it is private by default: Ala keeps it apart, only for delivering cards to you, and it never reaches anyone's phone, including the phone of the person who asked, unless you choose "Let them see my address". You can change or remove anything later from the same link. Everything entered through a link is removed with the link after 180 days.

How we use it

Only to run Ala: to sign you in, to save your cards, to print them at a kiosk and, where you've allowed it, to remind someone of your days. We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.

Measuring the website

The website uses Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages help people (for example, how many people open a card or find a kiosk). It sets small cookies in your browser for this. It does not tell us who you are. You can block these cookies in your browser and the website still works.

Who else handles it

They handle your data only to provide these services to us.

  • Amazon Web Services stores your saved cards and account, and each card-that-comes-alive's front picture and card details, on servers in India.
  • Google Cloud (Mumbai) stores secret videos, the days and addresses people add through Ala links, and the count of how often those cards are opened. Google Analytics counts website visits.
  • SMS and WhatsApp providers deliver your sign-in code.

How long we keep it

A card saved for printing at a kiosk is kept for 90 days after you last open it, then deleted automatically; opening it starts the 90 days again, and you can delete it any time with ✕ in My cards. Your account stays until you delete it. Records of cards you have bought are kept for as long as Indian tax law requires.

Deleting your account

In the app: Me > Delete my account. On the website: Me > Delete my account works the same way. We send a code to your number, and once you enter it your account is closed. You can also email us and we will delete it for you.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask to see, correct or erase your personal data, and you can withdraw your consent at any time. Email us to do any of these.

Children

If you are under 18, please use Ala with the consent of a parent or guardian.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update it here and change the date at the top.
