Anniversary cards with a photo of the two of you

A quarter of everything Ala prints is a love card: anniversaries, weddings, and cards for no reason at all.

The best ones are never the printed verse. They are a photo of the two of you and one line underneath that nobody else could have written. Pick an anniversary design, add the photo, write your line, and it prints on real card stock in about five minutes at an Ala kiosk inside a gift shop.

Chocolates get eaten and flowers fade. A card with your photo on it goes in a drawer and stays there for years. Find your nearest kiosk below.

Works just as well for parents' anniversaries: a wedding photo from thirty years ago on the front, and a line from the children inside. Or a card for no occasion at all, which is the kind people remember longest.
